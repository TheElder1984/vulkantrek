# Captain playtest

Use this protocol before tuning Captain resources or pacing. Automated pilots
already complete five seeded missions, but use exact targeting information and
cannot measure how readily a new player understands the bridge.

## Session

Start a new Captain mission with seed `1994`. Record the build date, operating
system, renderer and whether the player has played a command strategy game before.
Give the player the README's First encounter instructions and allow F1 help.
Ask them to clear the fleet before the deadline and decide whether to answer
the optional relief call. Avoid coaching unless they cannot continue; record
each intervention. Stop whenever the player wishes and record partial sessions.

Observe these moments without prescribing an approach:

- Preparing and executing the first order: do they understand the cost preview?
- First combat: do they understand shields and the shared laser/movement power?
- Leaving the opening quadrant: can they plot a valid course and find hostiles?
- First resupply: can they locate a base, approach it and dock?
- Relief alert: do they notice the marker and understand the countdown?
- Damaged life support: do they understand the travel warning and focused repair?
- Late mission: can they find the remaining fleet and understand the deadline?

Record elapsed real time and mission time at each observed moment, help lookups,
rejected orders, unexpected costs and any point where the player cannot identify
a next action. Distinguish a deliberate tactical risk from misunderstood rules.
Ask for a SAVE/RESTORE attempt during a quiet moment and confirm that the player
can resume their mission.

## Session record

Copy this block for each session; do not include personal identifying details.

```text
Build/date, OS, renderer:
Seed/rank:
Prior command-game experience:
Real minutes played / final mission day:
Outcome (win, loss, stopped) and reason:
Kills / remaining / crew:
Relief outcome:
First confusing action and expected behavior:
Help lookups / rejected orders / coaching:
Power shortages and resupply attempts:
Life-support warning and repair response:
SAVE/RESTORE result:
Text readability, sound and visual discomfort:
Most engaging moment / most repetitive stretch:
Would the player start another mission? Why?
```

## Follow-up

Repeat with seeds `7` and `42` after the opening is understood. Keep first-time
observations separate from experienced sessions. Fix reproducible misleading
previews, inaccessible controls or blocked progression first. Tune power,
deadlines or enemy strength only when the observations identify the cause of
difficulty. Record each rule change and rerun the simulation, campaign, bridge
and five-seed pilot checks listed in the README.

Human sessions have not yet been recorded. This protocol is preparation, not
evidence that pacing or accessibility has been validated.
